## Brand & Style

This design system captures the grandeur, warmth, and sacred opulence of a royal Indian wedding celebration. Rooted in heritage aesthetics reminiscent of Rajasthani palaces, hand-pressed gold-foil stationery, and celebratory nocturnal courtyards lit by brass diyas, the design balances timeless tradition with modern digital fluidity.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Regal & Opulent:** Elicits the awe of walking into a palace courtyard adorned with marigolds, silk drapery, and illuminated architecture.
- **Intimate & Auspicious:** Radiates blessings, warmth, family connection, and sacred joy (`Shubh Vivah`).
- **Refined & Graceful:** Modern high-luxury execution—never chaotic or kitsch, but artfully curated with intentional negative space, balanced ornamentation, and delicate metallic accents.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
The visual style is **Regal Heritage Luxury**: an expressive blend of traditional Indian architectural motifs (cusped jharokha arches, filigree patterns, fine gold-leaf rules) with refined high-contrast editorial layouts. Ambient diya glows and radiant gold foil overlays are rendered using sophisticated CSS linear/radial gradients and translucent layers, avoiding heavy skeuomorphism in favor of polished, tactile luxury.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from heritage royal nuptials: deep vermilion crimson, burnished gold leaf, luminous marigold, deep peacock navy, and artisanal ivory silk.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#800020` - Royal Vermilion / Deep Crimson):** Symbolizes auspiciousness, deep celebration, and timeless bridal luxury. Used for primary calls-to-action, structural headings, celebratory headers, and key focal boundaries.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Opulent Gold Foil):** Reflects beaten gold leaf and gilded calligraphy. Applied to delicate filigree borders, icons, monograms, interactive active states, and decorative divider accents.
- **Tertiary (`#E07A1E` - Royal Marigold / Genda Phool):** Introduces vibrancy, warmth, and festive vitality. Used for highlight tags, date callouts, ceremonial milestone markers, and diya flame glows.
- **Neutral Accent / Depth (`#0C1E36` - Deep Peacock Navy):** Acts as the foundational anchor for dark-tinted evening ceremony cards, contrast text, and celestial backgrounds for sangeet or reception segments.
- **Neutral Base (`#FAF6EE` - Ivory Cream / Raw Silk):** A warm, tactile cream background replacing clinical pure white, evoking handmade cotton rag paper and silk invitations.

### Surface Color Relationships
- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF6EE` with subtle warm undertones.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card Base):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#D4AF37` at 30% opacity.
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated State / Modal):** `#FDFBF7` with soft golden ambient drop shadows.
- **Surface Inverted (Nocturnal Events):** `#0C1E36` with `#D4AF37` gold typography and crimson badge accents.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy achieves a harmonious balance between royal ceremonial display and crystalline digital legibility.

### Font Pairings & Roles
- **Display & Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys the grace of custom engraved calligraphy and royal proclamations. It features high contrast serifs, classic letterforms, and romantic curves. It is used for couple names, event ceremony titles, shlokas/invitation verses, and section headlines.
- **Body & Secondary Copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a warm, crisp, and approachable sans-serif counterpart. Ensuring high readability across diverse screen sizes for event schedules, logistics, venue directions, and dress codes.
- **Accents & Subheaders:** Display labels make use of spaced small-caps or tracking-expanded `Playfair Display` (`0.08em` tracking) to replicate royal monogram detailing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an editorial fixed-fluid grid that mimics the deliberate framing of luxury invitations and illuminated manuscripts.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1280px` centered, allowing luxurious outer margins of `3rem` or greater. Visual components often center on 8-column spans to retain intimate, book-like proportions.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Elements adopt full width with internal card breathing space.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm utilizes deliberate pauses (`space-xl` and above) between timeline rituals (Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Pheras) to give each event ceremonial independence.
- Symmetrical spacing around ornaments and divider mandalas reinforces architectural balance.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than industrial drop shadows, depth is manifested through warm optical glow, layered paper stocks, and delicate metallic trims.

### Depth Paradigms
- **Diya Glow (Radiant Ambience):** Warm, amber-gold diffused glows (`box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18), 0 2px 8px 0 rgba(224, 122, 30, 0.12)`). Used on active ceremony cards, countdown modules, and primary celebration invitations.
- **Intricate Gold Filigree Borders:** Depth is demarcated via double-line borders combining a 1px solid `#D4AF37` (gold) with an inner inset border of 1px at 4px spacing, echoing Mughal jharokha framing.
- **Card Tiers:**
  - **Flat / Grounded:** Surface `#FFFFFF` on `#FAF6EE` with a 1px border of `#EADDC7`.
  - **Floating / Elevated:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with the ambient diya glow and an elevated gold rim.
  - **Royal Modal / Overlay:** Deep peacock navy (`#0C1E36`) with 40% backdrop blur and gold foil highlights.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, structured radius (`roundedness: 1`), complementing both classical architecture and modern digital controls.

### Architectural Archways & Corner Modifiers
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, inputs, notification badges) utilize `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`) to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner curvature.
- Signature visual containers (couple portraits, ceremony preview cards, hero frames) employ **Cusped Arch** or **Scalloped Arch** SVG masks or top-only rounding (`border-top-left-radius: 120px; border-top-right-radius: 120px;`) evoking traditional Indian palace entryways.
- Floating badges and date chips use smooth pill contours for balance.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Regal Button:** Deep vermilion (`#800020`) background, crisp metallic gold text (`#FFFFFF` or `#FAF6EE`), 1px solid border in `#D4AF37`. Hover states introduce an outward golden diya glow and subtle brightness shift.
- **Secondary / Gilded Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid gold foil border (`#D4AF37`), crimson text (`#800020`). Hover fills with soft ivory gold tint (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`).
- **Icon Buttons:** Circular or soft-squared with delicate gold outline and warm hover bloom.

### Event Cards & Ceremony Panels
- **Ceremony Timeline Card:** Encased in an ivory canvas with a jharokha-inspired top arch or corner gold brackets. Contains event title in `Playfair Display`, auspicious time in marigold accent (`#E07A1E`), venue details, and dress code tags.
- **Interactive Countdown Card:** Embedded glowing diya illustration, deep peacock navy or ivory container, framed in dual-line gold rules.

### Chips & Badges
- **Attire & Event Tags:** Soft cream background, `#800020` or `#0C1E36` text, micro gold border.
- **Status Badges (e.g., 'Auspicious Muhurat'):** Royal marigold background (`#E07A1E`) with crisp ivory typography.

### Input Fields & RSVP Forms
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Pure ivory background with warm beige base border (`#DECDB4`). On focus, border transitions to gleaming gold (`#D4AF37`) with a 3px soft ambient glow. Labels sit in high-contrast `Playfair Display` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Ornate circular/square selectors with gold accent borders, filled with crimson vermilion and a micro gold floral checkmark on selection.

### Custom Bespoke Elements
- **Mandala Section Dividers:** Symmetrical vector divider consisting of a central floral/mandala motif tapering into fine horizontal gold hairpins.
- **Blessings Guestbook Stream:** Fluid testimonial list wrapped in deckle-edge paper styled cards with gold wax-seal inspired avatar stamps.
