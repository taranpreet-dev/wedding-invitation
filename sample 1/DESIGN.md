## Brand & Style
The design system channels the grandeur, intimacy, and majestic hospitality of historic royal Indian weddings. It balances artisanal courtly heritage—reminiscent of Rajasthani havelis, Mughal arches, and hand-woven Benarasi silks—with a high-fashion, digital-first luxury editorial standard. The interface serves distinguished families and international guests navigating multi-day ceremonial festivities (Mehendi, Sangeet, Haldi, Baraat, Pheras, and Reception). 

The emotional tone evokes reverent awe, ceremonial warmth, exclusivity, and sacred celebration. Tactile skeuomorphism merges with regal editorial minimalism: surfaces glow with delicate gold foil framing, filigree dividers, arched portal framing, and silk-weave textural backdrops without sacrificing typographic clarity or accessibility.

## Colors
The color palette draws deeply from ancestral bridal trousseaus, ceremonial flora, and architectural palace stone:

- **Primary (`#780016` Deep Royal Burgundy Crimson)**: Represents royal vermilion and alta. Anchors primary calls-to-action, royal monogram seals, dominant mastheads, and high-emphasis focal items. Paired with deep inner burgundy (`#4A0E17`) for regal gradients and modal overlays.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` Luminous Wedding Gold)**: The hue of molten polki jewelry, zardozi embroidery, and warm temple lamps. Complemented by `#F5D77F` (champagne gold shimmer) for borders, active indicator rings, metallic sheen gradients, and ornate dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#1B4332` Deep Forest Emerald)**: Reminiscent of royal uncut emeralds and paan leaves; used sparingly for auspicious dates, affirmative states, venue navigation tags, and botanical accents.
- **Accents (Sacred Saffron Marigold - `#E65100` to `#FFA726`)**: Reserved for celebratory tags (e.g., Haldi rituals, sunrise ceremonies, live status tags).
- **Surface & Backgrounds (`#FDFBF7` Warm Ivory Silk to `#FFF9F2` Pearl Muslin)**: Replaces stark clinical white with natural organic raw silk, providing soft contrast against burgundy typography and gold filigree.

## Typography
Typographic scale hinges on intentional dualities: monumental, romantic high-contrast serifs for regal proclamation juxtaposed with razor-sharp, open humanist sans-serifs for itinerary clarity, transport coordination, and venue maps.

- **Playfair Display**: Evokes ceremonial royal scrolls, wedding invitation cards, and formal couple monograms. Rendered primarily in rich crimson (`#780016`) or deep antique bronze gold.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Manages all narrative reading, long-form descriptions of rituals (the significance of the *Saat Phere* or *Chooda* ceremony), and logistical guest guidance. Its geometric clarity balances the ornate serif.
- **Montserrat (Uppercase)**: Provides crisp, imperial gravitas to metadata, dates, venue codes, countdown timers, and button labels. High tracking (+0.08em to +0.18em) is strictly maintained for caps.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an architectural 12-column grid system for desktop and tablet landscape (1280px+ max container), shifting to a 6-column grid on tablet portrait (768px - 1024px), and a 4-column fluid layout on mobile (below 768px).

- **Margins & Air**: Spacing mimics royal invitations with broad, generous outer ivory margins (`space-xxl` on desktop mastheads) allowing ornate corner accents and border borders room to breathe without crowding editorial content.
- **Rhythm**: Sections alternate between airy light silk canvases and intimate, full-bleed burgundy or forest-emerald ceremonial cards. 
- **Alignment**: Central alignment reigns for ceremonial intros, royal crests, and date statements; asymmetric two-column layouts guide complex multi-day schedules and RSVP flows.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized through warm ambient glow and layered physical tiers rather than clinical synthetic drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base background sits on Warm Ivory Silk (`#FDFBF7`). Secondary cards rest on Pearl White Silk (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-fine metallic hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`).
- **Zari Glow & Ambient Shadows**: Floating elements (such as royal RSVP cards and date floating pills) utilize warm, tinted golden-umber shadows: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(120, 0, 22, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- **Gold Foil Inlay**: Modal dialogs and ceremonial cards feature an internal inset rim (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`) mimicking pressed gold foil bordering on heavy-weight handmade cardstock.
- **Backdrop Overlays**: Overlays employ a deep Burgundy-Noir wash (`rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.75)`) infused with a subtle 8px backdrop-blur to isolate modal focus while preserving ambient warmth.

## Shapes
The design adopts a delicate, soft structural form factor (`roundedness: 1`). Radii remain restrained (4px on controls, 8px on content containers, 12px on large ceremonial feature banners) to preserve the sharp, crisp precision of traditional royal invitations and stone-carved jali panels.

Distinctive signature shapes include:
- **Jharokha / Mandap Cusped Arches**: Featured as photo masks and header enclosures, giving event imagery an authentic regal architectural profile.
- **Gold Beveled Notches**: Occasional clipped-corner geometry on luxury badges and royal seal containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Regal Button**: Burgundy solid (`#780016`) background with luminous gold border (`1px solid #D4AF37`), gold foil text (`#FFF9F2`), styled in uppercase `label-button`. On hover, transitions to a rich inner glow with slight background shift to `#4A0E17` and elevated gold rim brightness (`#F5D77F`).
- **Secondary Royal Button**: Transparent canvas with a double gold border (1px hairline gold ring with 2px offset), text in burgundy (`#780016`), displaying a gold leaf glyph on hover.
- **Tertiary / Link Button**: Centered serif or all-caps text with a bespoke animated gold hairline underline that expands outward from center.

### Cards & Event Itinerary Modules
- **Ceremonial Day Cards**: Ivory or deep emerald containers framed in fine 1px gold filigree borders, topped with subtle decorative floral kalash or lotus icons. Includes ceremony time in `label-caps`, event name in `headline-md`, and location in `body-sm`.
- **Dress Code & Wardrobe Guidance Cards**: Minimalist portrait containers framed with cusped arch imagery cutaways, featuring emerald and gold tag ribbons (e.g., "Royal Formal / Bandhgala & Lehengas").

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags**: Elegant, slim capsules with `label-caps` typography. Haldi/Daytime uses `#FFA726` with deep amber text; Evening/Cocktail uses `#1B4332` with soft emerald-tinted gold text; Family/VIP holds a bordered burgundy badge.

### Input Fields & RSVP Form Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Ivory fields with bottom-border-only or delicate 1px border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)`). Floating labels utilize `label-meta` in antique crimson. Focus state transitions the border into luminous gold (`#D4AF37`) accompanied by an ambient champagne halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular rings with gold borders. On active selection, filled with an inner deep burgundy dot encased in a gilded rim.

### Dividers & Ceremonial Accents
- **Mandap & Paisley Dividers**: Horizontal layout rules feature a central botanical motif (lotus blossom or paisley droplet) flanked by tapered gold hairline flourishes.
- **Royal Monogram / Seal Component**: A circular double-gold ring enclosing the couple's intertwined initials in `Playfair Display`, used as section signatures and loading states.
