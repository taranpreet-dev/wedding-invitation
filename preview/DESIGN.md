## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the heritage, prestige, and bespoke romance of an elite ceremonial invitation atelier. Targeted at discerning couples, luxury wedding planners, and families curating grand multi-day celebrations, the experience balances majestic classical tradition with contemporary digital refinement.

The aesthetic philosophy draws upon **Haute Editorial Minimalism** layered with **Tactile Regal Romance**. Rather than cluttering the screen with faux-ornate skeuomorphism, the system prioritizes architectural white space (rendered in warm candlelight ivory), high-contrast royal burgundy hues, and champagne gold metallic accents. Interfaces should feel like flipping through a custom letterpress stationery folio or an haute couture lookbook: whisper-quiet elevations, razor-sharp hairline dividers, generous proportioning, and tactile typography that conveys permanence, dignity, and celebratory joy.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate atmosphere of ceremonial opulence rooted in warm, sunlit, and regal tones.

- **Primary (`#5B132B`):** Deep royal maroon. Used for primary typography, authoritative CTAs, luxury container headers, and grounding structural elements.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`):** Champagne gold. Serves as the luxury hallmark for metallic borders, active indicator stars, gilded pill badges, bespoke accents, and iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#721C39`):** Lighter royal burgundy. Provides subtle depth when layering interactive states, hover highlights on dark surfaces, or secondary badges.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2`):** Soft ivory. Serves as the primary canvas, replacing harsh digital whites with the warmth of handmade Italian deckle-edged paper.

### Additional Colors
- `deep-maroon` (`#570013`): An ultra-dense shadow maroon for hero titles and high-emphasis focal moments.
- `light-gold` (`#E5C378`): Luminous gold for subtle gradient highlights, gold leaf effects, and shimmer borders.
- `warm-parchment` (`#FDF9F1`): Card background fill that gently separates cards from the base ivory background.
- `border-linen` (`#ECE8E0`): Subdued, low-contrast hairline borders evocative of bound stationery.
- `whatsapp-emerald` (`#25D366`): Dedicated brand action color for high-conversion concierge and booking triggers, applied thoughtfully to preserve the atelier aesthetic without overpowering the gold-maroon harmony.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a classical dialogue between romantic serif display titles and airy, contemporary sans-serif metadata.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys bespoke calligraphy, historical refinement, and ceremony. Use italic variations sparingly for romantic emphasis (e.g., *Bespoke Suites*, *The Royal Collection*).
- **Body & Captions (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its geometric clarity and gentle warmth. Balances the dramatic weight of the serif headlines, ensuring product descriptions, materials notes, and pricing tables remain effortlessly legible.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans` Uppercase):** Category badges, collection dividers, and button labels leverage all-caps treatment with wide character tracking (`0.08em` - `0.12em`) to mirror luxury catalog typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an editorial, magazine-inspired grid that emphasizes balance, breathing room, and intentional asymmetrical showcases.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive layout on desktop with a maximum canvas width of `1320px`. Shifts to 6 columns on tablet devices (`768px - 1024px`) and a single/two-column hybrid rhythm on mobile screens (`< 768px`).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` and section margins up to `6rem`) prevent cognitive clutter, treating each invitation suite as a gallery piece. Catalog listings employ staggered heights or masonry card groupings to simulate physical invitation suites laid out for review.
- **Form Factor Transitions:** On desktop, large visual previews take center-left dominance with specifications anchored right. On mobile devices, layouts stack vertically with sticky concierge booking actions pinned to the viewport base.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh digital drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering**, **Warm Ambient Diffusion**, and **Hairline Foil Outlines**.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Uncoated `#FAF7F2` soft ivory.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):** `#FDF9F1` warm parchment framed with a `1px` border of `#ECE8E0` or a soft gold sheen (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Showcase Cards):** Elevated with an ambient, golden-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(91, 19, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Full-Screen Suite Previews):** Deep backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with a rich overlay (`rgba(87, 0, 19, 0.5)`), paired with a floating panel cast in `box-shadow: 0 24px 64px -12px rgba(45, 8, 20, 0.35)`.
- **Gilded Borders:** Interactive focal components utilize delicate double-borders or inset metallic outlines (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`) that evoke gold-leaf debossing.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft, Tailored Silhouette (`roundedness: 1`)**, reflecting heavy artisanal cardstock, cut paper edges, and envelope flaps rather than bubbly app-like curves.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `0.25rem` (`4px`) to `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius. This subtle softening preserves geometric precision while avoiding harsh needles at card corners.
- **Modals & Preview Panels:** Retain `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) corners to evoke custom presentation keepsake boxes.
- **Badges, Pills & Icon Actions:** May selectively adopt full pill shapes (`rounded-full`) exclusively for tags (e.g., "Handmade Paper", "Gold Foil", "Save the Date") and floating action triggers to create visual contrast against rectangular stationery cards.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Atelier Button:** Solid royal maroon (`#5B132B`) fill with warm ivory text (`#FAF7F2`), subtle gold border highlight, and uppercase tracked text (`label-md`). Hover state gently shifts to `#721C39` with an elevated gold ambient glow.
- **Secondary Foil Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#D4AF37`, rich maroon text. Hover applies a soft gold wash (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`).
- **WhatsApp Concierge Booking Button:** Distinctive pill button featuring `#25D366` green background or white pill with vibrant emerald WhatsApp glyph and maroon typography, framed with an optional pulse or gold badge tag reading "Instant Design Consultation".

### Badges & Chips
- **Bespoke Status Chips:** Rendered in `#FAF7F2` with `1px` borders in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`. Text in `#5B132B` at `label-sm` with letter spacing `0.1em`.
- **Prestige Tags (e.g., "Limited Edition", "Wax Sealed"):** Rich burgundy background (`#5B132B`) with shimmering champagne text (`#E5C378`).

### Product & Suite Showcase Cards
- Encased in `#FDF9F1` with an inner hairline frame simulating a matted photo mount.
- Card media features an interactive "Quick Look" foil badge on hover.
- Card footers contain suite title in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, price tier, printing technique badges, and a direct "Enquire via WhatsApp" icon trigger.

### Interactive Preview Modals
- High-fidelity lightboxes allowing full magnification of invitation suites, envelope liners, RSVP cards, and calligraphy detail.
- Lightbox controls feature slim champagne gold icons, deckle-edge sheet toggles, and direct sample request forms.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background in `#FAF7F2` with bottom-line underline styling or crisp `1px` linen borders (`#ECE8E0`).
- Focus state transforms border into crisp champagne gold (`#D4AF37`) with zero harsh outer halo.
- Floating serif labels transition to `label-sm` upon entry.

### Radio Buttons & Selection Cards (Paper / Print Customizers)
- Custom square or round selectors finished with a central gold leaf dot upon selection.
- Selection cards for paper weights (e.g., 600 GSM Cotton, Deckle Edge, Velvet) feature tactile thumbnail previews with gilded active outlines.
