# Premium Wedding Invitation — Tap & Reveal

A mobile-first Indian wedding invitation built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

## Interaction
- Envelope seal opens the invitation.
- Envelope flap tilts back and the paper rises.
- Smooth transition pulls the visitor into the invitation.
- Music attempts to start from the seal click, subject to browser autoplay rules.
- Top-right music button controls playback.
- Three elegant cards use a real tap-to-reveal 3D flip:
  - Wedding date/time
  - Venue/address
  - Invitation message
- Moving butterflies fly around the page and disappear when touched.
- Every tap/touch creates a short golden glow and sparkle burst.
- Countdown, gallery lightbox, events, map, RSVP and contact actions work.
